# MASS — 30s cinematic film · storyboard

**Format:** 1920×1080, 24 fps, 30.0 s (720 frames). Stereo 48 kHz.
**Frame:** Acts I–II are letterboxed at 2.39:1. At the cut to Earth the bars
retract to full 16:9: the frame opens up as the borders do.
**Look:** real photography graded like a documentary feature: soft highlight roll-off,
halation, 35mm grain, slow camera moves with handheld drift. The digital layer is
always crisp light: hairlines, glass panels and type in the Mass palette.
**Type:** Act I is typed in *Courier Prime*, the typewriter voice of the paper era.
From "Until now." on, everything is *Inter Display*, the clean voice of the website.
Data labels use *JetBrains Mono*.
**Palette** (sampled from mass.inc): ink `#07040E`, gold `#FFD88A`, text `#E8E8EC`,
grey `#8A8A94`, label `#5A5A62`. Act I only: stamp red `#B8322A`.

The story in one breath: *every economy runs on companies, and companies still live
on paper, in one office, in one country. Mass turns the company into a live digital
record, formed in minutes, checked as code and held under government keys.
Connect the nations that run it and you get one economy.*

### Why this order works
1. **Stakes first.** Paper, stamps, queues and borders are universal pain, so everyone
   recognises it in under eight seconds.
2. **One line of contrast.** "Until now." is the pivot, after the only silence in the film.
3. **Concrete before abstract.** We show one founder's company being born before
   explaining layers, then trade, then the planet.
4. **Scale as the payoff.** The frame widens as the network grows, so the "aha" lands
   visually: every nation keeps its own keys, and together they form one economy.

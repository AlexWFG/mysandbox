# Assurance — "Making lies expensive" · storyboard

Source: `Propchain — Assurance Fraud, Bonds and Insurance.pdf` (concept note, working draft, Sept 2026).
Target length ≈ 3:40. Narrator George (series settings). Picture is timed to the narration; beat times
below are planned and get replaced by measured VO timings in `film/timeline.json`.

## What the viewer must understand (learning goals)

1. **Attestation proves provenance, not truth.** A signed record can still be a lie; consensus can't tell.
   The money in fraud is misstated income and diverted closing funds, and both have a mechanical truth: cash.
2. **Defence in layers.** Six layers (identity, provenance, corroboration, economic, professional, memory),
   each stopping what the one below cannot; the residual is what insurance prices.
3. **Corroboration with cash as tie-break.** Independent sources = independent *incentives*. Worked NOI
   example: €1,020k / €1,020k / €958k / €955k → quorum €956k at 2 % tolerance → level A3.
4. **Assurance levels A0–A5**, per field, required by counterparties; A4 needs capital at risk.
5. **The bond, with the deck's numbers.** €13.0M price, €800k NOI, 6.15 % cap rate; warranty ±5 % for
   12 months (€40k tolerance); bond = €80k ÷ 6.15 % = €1.3M (10 % of price) in yield-bearing escrow;
   trigger = 12 months of bank receipts; €760k → released in full; €720k → half to the buyer (pro rata).
6. **What can be slashed:** objective (bondable) / adjudicated (bondable with a named referee) /
   subjective (never). Staking's lesson.
7. **Who posts capital:** usually not new money (replaces holdback / retention / W&I). Self-bond, surety,
   policy; the market is the surety book. The certainty-premium test, which must be measured.
8. **The insurer:** A data partner → B MGA (realistic route) → C own carrier (later). The edge is
   claimed-vs-realised observation = a loss curve. Short-tail parametric products first; float last.
9. **The plan:** Measure first. Bond what is measured. Insure the rest. (Working draft; illustrative numbers.)

## Arc

Problem (a signed lie) → the layered defence → corroboration (cheap, informational) → the bond (economic:
capital at risk, worked through both outcomes) → where bonds stop working → who actually posts →
the insurer that prices what's left → the plan.

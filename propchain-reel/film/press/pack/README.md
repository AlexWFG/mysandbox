# Propchain press pack: tokenization hype vs. reality (2022–2026)

Everything here was captured from live, public pages in Chrome on 26 Sep 2026. On every cookie banner I clicked Reject or Necessary only. Headlines and quotes are copied verbatim from the page. Key quotes are kept short (under about 15 words); the full context is in the screenshots.

## Files
- `screenshots/NN_outlet_hero.png` shows the headline, standfirst, byline, date and outlet logo.
- `screenshots/NN_outlet_detail.png` shows the key paragraph or pull quote, centred in the frame.
- `screenshots/NN_outlet_full.png` is a scroll-stitched capture of the article, for scroll-through animation.
- `screenshots/18_outliermedia_detail2.png` is a second detail frame for item 18.
- `press_manifest.csv` has 20 articles in chronological order.
- `video_moments.csv` has 17 video moments. 11 of them have timestamps checked against the YouTube transcript.

## Capture specs and limitations
- The screen is 1512 px wide with the Claude side panel open, so frames are about 1304×924 (items 01–07 are 1512×787), not 1920×1080. Page zoom is 115% except where noted in the notes column: Fox, Outlier and DLD are at 100–125%.
- Screenshots come from JPEG frames and were saved as PNG.
- Very long pages have a partial full-page capture, which covers the top of the article through the key section. These are McKinsey, J.P. Morgan, Bloomberg/Yahoo, Outlier (18), IMF, and the BlackRock letter (tokenization section only). The notes column says which.
- These ads or widgets could not be removed: the Decrypt sidebar prediction widget; Fox Business display ads in the full-page capture; sidebar video players on CoinDesk and Forbes.
- The Forbes membership pop-up was closed with "No Thanks". The BlackRock site-terms pop-up was accepted with your approval. No paywall was bypassed. Bloomberg item 10 was captured from its free Yahoo Finance syndication.
